Signs you need it

If two or more of these sound familiar, get it checked before the next storm.

  • The attic is over 140°F on summer afternoons
  • Upstairs rooms stay hot no matter the thermostat
  • Shingles curling or blistering early
  • Mold or a musty smell in the attic
  • Insulation blocking the soffits

How we do it, step by step

No mystery. This is exactly what happens, in order.

  1. Measure the atticFloor area and current vents tell us how much intake and exhaust you need.
  2. Balance intake and exhaustAir comes in at the soffits and leaves at the ridge; both sides have to match.
  3. Install bafflesFoam baffles keep insulation from blocking airflow at the eaves.
  4. Cut and cap the ridgeA continuous ridge vent with a weather filter.

Attic ventilation questions

Does more venting always help?

No. Unbalanced vents can pull rain in. We balance intake and exhaust.

Will it lower my power bill?

A cooler attic means the AC works less; savings depend on your insulation and ducts.

Can you add it without a new roof?

Yes, ridge vents can be cut into most existing shingle roofs.
