Leaking right now?

Do these four things tonight

Most leak damage happens in the first few hours, inside the house. None of this needs a roofer.

Text us a photo
  1. Catch it and move thingsPut a bucket under the drip and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way.
  2. Drain a bulging ceilingIf drywall is sagging with water, poke a small hole at the lowest point over the bucket. It keeps the whole ceiling from coming down.
  3. Take photos nowPhotograph the stain, the drip and anything wet. Your insurance company will ask for them.
  4. Stay off the roofWet shingles are slick, even for pros. We tarp roofs after storms, usually the same or next day.

What’s under your roof

  1. Looks fine from the streetA 15-year-old roof can look normal from the driveway. Those dark streaks are where the granules are gone. Keep scrolling.
  2. What the tear-off foundUnder the old shingles: torn felt and a big patch of soft, rotted wood. This is what was leaking into the attic.
  3. We cut out the rotEvery soft board comes out and new decking goes in. We text you photos before anything covers it.
  4. Waterproof layersIce and water shield along the edge, then synthetic underlayment that keeps the deck dry even if a shingle blows off.
  5. New impact shinglesClass 4 shingles with six nails each, a ridge vent on top and new metal drip edge.
  6. Storm readySame roof, first big storm. Water runs off into the gutter instead of into the house.

Questions people ask us

How close is the roof check price to the real one?

For most homes it lands within 10 percent. The free inspection confirms the size, the pitch and whether any decking needs replacing before you sign anything.

Will insurance pay for a new roof?

If hail or wind damaged it, often yes. We document the damage with photos and meet your adjuster. You pay your deductible if the claim is approved.

How long does a new roof take?

One or two days for most homes, a little longer if we find rotted decking. We text you before we start and when we finish.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. Ask at your inspection and we’ll show you monthly payment options.
