How much does a roof replacement cost in Houston?

Most Houston homes land between $9,000 and $20,000 for architectural shingles, depending on size, slope and layers. Use the instant quote above for your home, then we confirm it at a free inspection.

Does homeowners insurance cover roof replacement in Texas?

Usually when the damage comes from a covered event like hail or wind, not age or wear. We document the damage and meet your adjuster. You pay your deductible; it’s illegal in Texas for a roofer to waive it.

Repair or replace: how do I know?

If the roof is under about 15 years old and the problem is in one spot, a repair is usually enough. Older roofs with widespread wear or soft decking usually need replacing. The inspection photos make it clear.

Do you do emergency roof repair and tarping?

Yes. After storms we tarp the same or next day to stop water coming in.

Do I need a permit for a new roof in Houston?

Yes, the City of Houston requires a re-roof permit and inspection. We pull it for you.
